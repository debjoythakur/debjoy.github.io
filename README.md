# Debjoy Thakur, Ph.D.

Welcome to my academic website and research repository.

I am an **Assistant Professor at Ahmedabad University, India**, working in **Spatial Statistics, Statistical Learning, Spatial Data Science, and Machine Learning**. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Lecturer in the Department of Statistics and Data Science at Washington University in St. Louis.

This repository hosts my academic website and provides information about my **research, publications, teaching, research software and code, and curriculum vitae**.

## Research Interests

My research interests include:

* High-dimensional spatial variable selection
* Statistical learning theory
* Spatial and spatio-temporal statistics
* Spatial extremes
* Spatial genomics and fMRI data analysis
* Network data analysis
* Variational inference
* Deep learning for spatial data

A major focus of my research is the development of statistical learning methods that account for **spatial heterogeneity, nonstationarity, multiresolution structure, high dimensionality, and complex spatial dependence**.

## Research

My current research spans several interconnected areas of spatial statistics and statistical learning.

* **Localized Variable Selection:** Development of localized LASSO, SCAD, and multiresolution variable-selection methods for spatially varying relationships.

* **Spatial Point Processes:** High-dimensional and localized modeling of spatial intensity functions with applications to crime, transportation, and environmental data.

* **Spatial Extremes:** Statistical and machine-learning methodology for environmental extremes, threshold exceedances, and nonstationary risk surfaces.

* **Spatial Deep Learning:** Neural-network and graph-based approaches incorporating spatial dependence, covariance structure, and local information.

* **Spatial Data on Manifolds:** Intensity and pair-correlation estimation for spatial point processes observed on Riemannian manifolds.

* **Variational Inference:** Computationally scalable variational methods for spatial models and restricted maximum likelihood estimation.

* **Spatial Genomics:** Feature-selection and statistical-learning methods for irregularly spaced spatial transcriptomics and other high-dimensional spatial biomedical data.

## Research Code

Reproducible code for selected research projects is maintained in separate GitHub repositories.

### Multi-Resolution Variable Selection for Spatial Point Processes

[Multi-Resolution-Variable-Selection-Spatial-Point-Process](https://github.com/debjoythakur/Multi-Resolution-Variable-Selection-Spatial-Point-Process)

Code associated with multiresolution variable selection for spatial point-process intensity models, with applications to spatial event data in St. Louis.

### Local Variable and Neighborhood Selection

[-Local-Variable-and-Neighborhood-Selection-for-Firearm-Fatality-in-South-East-USA](https://github.com/debjoythakur/-Local-Variable-and-Neighborhood-Selection-for-Firearm-Fatality-in-South-East-USA)

Code associated with localized variable and neighborhood selection for spatial firearm-fatality data in the southeastern United States.

### Variational REML for Spatial Data

[Variational-REML-Spatial](https://github.com/debjoythakur/Variational-REML-Spatial)

Code associated with variational restricted maximum likelihood estimation for spatial models, including scalable variational approximation for spatial random effects.
