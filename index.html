Research

Research themes

Spatial Variable Selection

Localized and multiresolution variable-selection methods for spatially heterogeneous regression and point-process models.

Spatial Extremes

Statistical and machine-learning methods for environmental extremes, threshold exceedances, and nonstationary risk surfaces.

Statistical Learning Theory

Theoretical foundations for localized learning, spatial dependence, penalization, and asymptotic guarantees.

Variational Inference

Scalable variational approximations for latent spatial models, including restricted likelihood and structured random effects.

Spatial Genomics & fMRI

Methods for high-dimensional spatially structured biomedical and imaging data.

Network & Spatial AI

Graph-based, network-aware, and deep-learning methods for complex spatial systems and transportation data.
